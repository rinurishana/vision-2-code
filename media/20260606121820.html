About Regional Technologies

Established in the vibrant city of Kozhikode, Regional Technologies stands as a beacon for innovation and development. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and practical application, providing a dynamic platform for students and professionals to develop cutting-edge projects.

We specialize in nurturing talent across various technological domains, including software development, hardware prototyping, data science, and AI. Our centre is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and mentored by experienced industry experts, ensuring a conducive environment for learning, experimentation, and project execution.

At Regional Technologies, we believe in empowering the next generation of innovators and contributing significantly to the technological landscape of Kozhikode and beyond.
